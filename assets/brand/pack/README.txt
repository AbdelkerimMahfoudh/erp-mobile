DUKAN ERP | Purple logo asset pack

Logo/ contains transparent artwork for dark backgrounds, as SVG paths and PNG.
Icon/Home-icon-1024.png is the opaque square home icon. Its full-bleed navy background allows the device to apply its own corner mask.
The SVGs are traced from the approved purple proof and use flat fills. Accent #5146D9, launch navy #000A37, icon navy #0B1D59, lettering #FFFFFF.

Motion/ contains three SVG layers sharing the full wordmark canvas. Overlay them at the same size. UKAN is one layer to preserve the chosen K–A join. Open Replay.html in a browser for a standalone demo. The preview fades to navy where the app screen would appear.

Launch timing, 1.2 seconds total
0–150 ms: D centered on navy.
150–800 ms: D eases left as UKAN slides right and is revealed from left to right.
700–950 ms: smaller ERP fades in beside N.
950–1200 ms: brief hold, then fade into the ready app screen.
